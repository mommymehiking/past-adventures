Niles Canyon Railway Holiday Train of Lights!

Toddler friendlyKids & familiesEast Bay

Tuesday, Dec. 2, 4:15 — 6:30 p.m.


Roll into the holiday season with this beloved Bay Area holiday event: the Niles Canyon Railway Holiday Train of Lights! These tickets are EXTREMELY DIFFICULT TO BOOK, so for a second year in a row,&nbsp;we have CHARTERED THE ENTIRE TRAIN!

This year, we’ll welcome 10 adults and children for free under our EVERY 10th program.

About the outing, from the railway's website:&nbsp;

Enjoy a 1.5-hour-long round trip through Niles Canyon filled with refreshments, music, and cheer aboard our antique coaches and open cars that feature holiday lights and decorations inside and out.

Our antique coaches and our open cars are&nbsp;decorated for the season, inside and out, and we enjoy&nbsp;holiday music&nbsp;on our 90-minute trip. We have some&nbsp;open cars with roofs&nbsp;where you can enjoy some of the&nbsp;best views of the canyon&nbsp;as our train makes the 90-minute round trip to Sunol and back.

The train leaves from Niles/Fremont Station at 37029 Mission Boulevard, Fremont.

*If you have any questions about the charter, you can contact&nbsp;Donna Alexander&nbsp;at Niles Canyon Railway:&nbsp;510-996-8420 or&nbsp;station-agent@ncry.org

ALSO, included in your ticket:
— Special treats for each child
— A fun family scavenger hunt
— Art activity to take home
— Our digital guide to make your experience seamless and magical
— Guaranteed seats without the stress of fighting for tickets online
