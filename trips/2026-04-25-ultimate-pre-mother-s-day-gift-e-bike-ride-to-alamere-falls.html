Ever wished you could experience Alamere Falls in Point Reyes — but the 13-mile hike felt like too much? Now you can. 🙌🏽

Alamere Falls is one of the most breathtaking places in California ✨ — a rare tidefall where a waterfall cascades directly onto the beach, the Pacific crashing at your feet, dramatic cliffs rising above you. Most people never make it there because the only way in is a long, challenging hike — until now.

E-bikes have changed everything. 🚲⚡ We'll ride through the spectacular coastal trails of Point Reyes National Seashore — arriving at the beach around low tide for the most magical waterfall experience possible. The e-bikes handle the hills so you can focus on the views, the conversation, and the pure joy of the ride.

Come solo and leave with a crew, or bring your adventure buddy and make it a day you'll never forget. 👯‍♀️ This is the ultimate pre-Mother's Day gift to yourself — and to every mom who shows up alongside you. Spots are limited — let's ride. 🚲🌊🌸

WHAT MAKES THIS RIDE SO SPECIAL:

🚲 E-Bikes Open the Way — You Bring the Adventure

Think wide open road, not a narrow single track through the woods. 🛣️ The route follows a mix of pavement and dirt-and-gravel fire road with sustained climbs and descents — intermediate difficulty.

But here's what changes everything: the electric assist means the climbs that would stop most people on a regular bike become genuinely doable. ⚡💪🏽 You still need to be comfortable on a bike and confident going downhill — this isn't a leisurely beach cruise.

But if you've wanted to experience Alamere Falls and the 13-mile hike always felt like too much, e-bikes make this the ride for you. We're partnering with an expert e-bike vendor whose guides will have you briefed and rolling within 20 minutes — and they, plus FAS, will be with you the whole way. 🙌🏾

🌊 California's Rarest Waterfall — Timed Perfectly

Alamere Falls is one of only TWO tidefalls in the entire state. We've dialed in our arrival around low tide so you get the full experience: waterfall mist on your face, ocean at your feet, cliffs towering above. 🏔️ Most people never see this. You will. ✨

🤝 Bring Your Adventure Buddy

🌸 Point Reyes at Its Most Beautiful

Late April is peak wildflower season 🌼🌺💜 — golden poppies, purple lupine, and coastal paintbrush blanketing the hills. Tule elk grazing. 🦌 The dramatic cliffs. The endless blue horizon. It's the kind of scenery that fills you with pure joy!

🚲 About This Ride &amp; Fitness

Good news: e-bikes are a game changer! ⚡ The electric assist handles the climbing so you can focus on the stunning scenery and the joy of the ride. This is a wide, open fire road shared with hikers, equestrians and the occasional ranger in a vehicle — not a technical mountain trail.

That said, this is a real ride and we want everyone to have an amazing experience. The route is 17+ miles with sustained climbs and descents on a mix of pavement and dirt/gravel fire road — intermediate difficulty. You don't need to be super fit, but you do need to have ridden a bike recently and feel confident handling one on varied terrain.

When we arrive at Wildcat Beach, we're not just stopping for photos 📸 — we're stopping to truly soak it all in. 🌊✨

We'll have guided journal prompts waiting for you at the waterfall 📝 — a moment to reflect, create, and just be somewhere extraordinary. The Pacific at your feet. The waterfall behind you. Nowhere else to be.

This time is YOURS. 💛 Bring your own journal; we'll bring the prompts and simple art supplies. No agenda. No rushing. Just you, the falls, and whatever comes up when you finally give yourself permission to stop. 🎨💭🌊
