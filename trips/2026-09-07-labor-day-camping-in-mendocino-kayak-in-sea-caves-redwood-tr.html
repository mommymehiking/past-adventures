LABOR DAY CAMPING in MENDOCINO: Kayak in Sea Caves 🛶 Redwood Train Ride 🌲🚂 Sea Glass 💎 Beach Play 🏖️ + More

Friday, Sep 4 — Monday, Sep 7, 2026

A Labor Day weekend for the memory books in Mendocino County:&nbsp;

WHAT WE'LL ENJOY&nbsp;🥳🥳🥳

🛶🐚 KAYAK THROUGH SEA CAVES: Paddle right into hidden sea caves and past lounging harbor seals — family-friendly, no experience needed, and honestly one of the coolest things you'll do all year.

🚂🌲🚂 RIDE THE REDWOOD TRAIN: Chug through a towering redwood forest on a family-friendly train ride departing from Fort Bragg — kids (and adults) will love it.

💦🥾 CHASE A LOCAL WATERFALL: We'll hike out to see a local waterfall in full flow (fingers crossed on rainfall!) — if it's running low by September, we'll pivot to a lighthouse + harbor seal viewing outing instead, just as magical.

🪸⭐🐚 EXPLORE TIDEPOOLS: Get up close with starfish, anemones, and crabs in the tidepools — a hit with every age group.

🌊🏖️ BEACH DAYS, MENDOCINO &amp; FORT BRAGG: Multiple beach windows built into the weekend, plus time to wander Mendocino village, Fort Bragg, and Glass Beach's famous sea glass.

🏕️🌲🏖️ CAMP IN A COASTAL FOREST: Home base is a stunning forest campground just 2 miles north of Mendocino — fall asleep to the sound of the ocean through the trees.

🫶🏿🫶🏾🫶🏽 COMMUNITY, FRIENDSHIP &amp; SUPPORT: Instant 'playdate' for your kiddos! We usually have kids from toddlers up to tweens join, with all of the kids playing together! You might see old MMH friends + hopefully make some new ones, too! MMH offers all kinds of support: Need help setting up your tent? Solo parenting on the adventure and need some extra support? We got you!

This is our idea of ADVENTURE CAMP for kids, but it's for families and we get to explore different campgrounds and parks! You will make wonderful memories with your family and friends&nbsp;🏕️❤️🫂🤲🏽!!

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • Guided outings!!!

  • Camp in a group with other families

  • Enjoy an educational scavenger hunt and adventure maps with your kids!

  • Make s’mores around the campfire (gas stove if campfire is not allowed at the campground; depends on what authorities decide)

  • Toy and arts-and-craft station, plus outdoor adventure lab, for the kids when we’re not out adventuring

  • Make wonderful memories with your family and friends
