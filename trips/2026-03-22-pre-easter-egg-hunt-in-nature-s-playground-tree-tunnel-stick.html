Pre-Easter Egg Hunt in Nature's Playground: Tree Tunnel! Stick Forts! Stumps + Logs! Beach! Wildflowers

Day tripHikingToddler friendlyKids & families

Sunday, March 22, 10:30 A.M.&nbsp;&nbsp;

Do you know this coastal preserve? It's another hidden gem that offers a nature's playground for the littles: a tree tunnel, stick forts, stumps and long, long logs to climb on, and the beach to explore for crab shells and more! We might see a horse or two walking on the beach as well.

There are SO many places to hide the treats — this is a perfect spot for it! I've included in the sign-up form a question about what kinds of treats are suitable. This is a NON-DENOMINATIONAL Easter Egg hunt.

IT WILL BE THE PERFECT SUNDAY ON THE COAST:

🐣🧺🪵 Roam through an awesome nearly half-mile long tree tunnel while the kids do the Easter Egg/scavenger hunt and play in the stick forts and on the stumps and logs!
🏖️🐚🪣 Relax on the beach, play with your kids on the shore, collect seashells and/or build sandcastles
🐴🐦‍⬛🌸 Look for all manner of critters, the season's first wildflowers! Maybe see horses trotting on the beach, watch a murmuration of red-winged blackbirds (amazing!) or a flock of pelicans diving for food in the ocean!
🏕️❤️🫂&nbsp;Community, friendship + support:&nbsp;Instant 'playdate' for your kiddos! We usually have kids from toddlers up to tweens join, with all of the kids playing together. You might see old MMH friends + hopefully make some new ones, too! We also offer all kinds of support: Forgot baby wipes? Need more snacks? Solo parenting on the outing and need some extra help? We got you!
📖🔍🗺️Explore part of the Bay Area!&nbsp;We will include tips on what to see in this part of the Bay Area to make the outing more of a day trip adventure, if you like!
