May 10, 2025

A Pre-Mother's Day Adventure: Pony Ride, Barn + Horse Visit, Tree Play, Wildflowers + Beach!

Day tripHikingToddler friendlyKids & families

Saturday, May 10, 10:30 a.m.

Celebrating moms with a Pre-Mother’s Day adventure like no other!

WHAT TO EXPECT

🐴🐴🐴 PONY RIDE: Kids will get to ride two laps around an arena on a pony with a parent on the sidelines! Families can take a photo with their little on a pony with the OCEAN as a stunning background.

🐴🤠🐐 BARN TOUR: The stables will take families on a guided tour of the barn! You’ll meet the horses, many of them rescues. Kids will get to pet and feed the horses. Plus, kids will get to check out two miniature goats + the antique tractor, too!

👩🏿‍🍼🫶🏿👩🏽‍🍼🫶🏿👩🏼‍🍼🫶🏿 CELEBRATING MOMS: Moms will get a goody bag with self care treats for them on their special day!

🌲🪵🌲 CLIMB: Many cypress trees + logs to climb + explore!

🏖️🐚🧺 BEACH PICNIC: A short hike will take us to a stunning beach with shells, sand, tiny fossils and water! We’ll picnic, play + explore this hidden gem!

🌸🌼🪻WILDFLOWERS/BERRIES: SPRING-A-LING: Buttery buttercups and vanilla-and-fushia colored ice plants dot the hillsides of this hidden gem! Blackberries + strawberries are popping up, we’ll see if any are ready to sample! Enjoy the flowers and the beautiful shades of green everywhere

🏕️❤️🫂 Community, friendship + support: Instant 'playdate' for your kiddos! We usually have kids from toddlers up to tweens join, with all of the kids playing together. You might see old MMH friends + hopefully make some new ones, too! We also offer all kinds of support: Forgot baby wipes? Need more snacks? Solo parenting and need some extra help? We got you!

📖🔍🗺️Explore part of the Bay Area! We will include tips on what to see in this part of the Bay Area to make the outing more of a day trip adventure, if you like!
