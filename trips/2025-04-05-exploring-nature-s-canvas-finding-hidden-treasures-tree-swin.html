Exploring Nature's Canvas: Finding Hidden Treasures, Tree Swings, Climbing Rocks + More in Beach Paradise

HikingKids & familiesEast BayMarin & Point ReyesSan Francisco

Saturday, April 5, 10:30 a.m.

In the East Bay is a park, on the edge of San Francisco Bay, where kid-friendly artwork mixes with nature’s artistry, offering so many gems to discover — some popping up from out of the ground! As we journey through this unique landscape, we’ll uncover its hidden treasures and the children — and parents, too — will hopefully discover, or rediscover their inner artist! And we won’t just see art …

Highlights include:

  • Swinging on tree swings! Including a tire swing

  • Exploring art that’s become part of the nature in this park by the San Francisco Bay

  • Playing in water, building sandcastles or flying kites on a serene beach!

  • Climbing rocks, hopping on and off tree stumps

  • Looking for bugs, like slugs, and butterflies!

  • Families making their own artwork to take home!

  • Breathtaking views of San Francisco, the Golden Gate, and Marin

  • Admiring end-of-season wildflowers

  • Exploring historic military bunkers

  • Spotting over 150 species of birds
