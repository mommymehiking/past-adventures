MOTHER'S DAY SUMMIT HIKE: A Moms-Only Adventure in a Coastal Paradise

Day tripHikingMoms onlyKids & families

Sunday, May 11, 2 p.m.

Moms, imagine standing atop this local summit, breathing in crisp ocean air as you gaze across the expansive blue horizon. Picture yourself wandering through meadows dotted with all kinds of wildflowers: California poppies, Indian paintbrush, sticky monkey, lupines and wild iris. Feel the satisfaction of conquering the trail alongside women who understand your journey, followed by the warm camaraderie of a well-earned meal where stories and laughter flow freely.

Escape the everyday and embrace the extraordinary this Mother's Day with our moms-only summit hike through the breathtaking landscapes and views of this coastal park.


WHAT TO EXPECT

  • 🌲 Challenge yourself on the scenic trail to a summit, where each step upward mirrors the strength you summon daily as a mother—navigating steep moments with perseverance and discovering breathtaking vistas that remind you why the journey matters

  • 👩🏿‍🍼🫶🏿Connect deeply with a circle of fellow mothers while sharing stories along the trail, participating in guided viewpoint reflections, and exchanging perspectives that honor each woman's unique path

  • Create space for personal renewal through mindful hiking practices, guided summit meditation, and moments of solo contemplation

  • 👩🏽‍🍼🫶🏿 Celebrate your strength with simple yet meaningful rituals including the summit stone ceremony where you claim a word that represents your power, the gratitude circle that acknowledges your growth, and the liberating mountain call that invites you to be heard in your fullness

  • 🌸🌼🪻Explore natural wonders with our knowledgeable guide pointing out spring wildflowers, coastal ecology and the fascinating natural history that makes this park special

  • Indulge yourself with a delicious post-hike dinner at a local restaurant, toasting to new and old friendships and renewed spirits

  • 👩🏼‍🍼🫶🏿 Commemorate Mother's Day your way—honoring your authentic self with photographs at scenic vistas, giving yourself permission to receive rather than always give

  • 📖🔍🗺️Explore part of the Bay Area! We will include tips on what to see in this part of the Bay Area to make the outing more of a day trip adventure, if you like!
