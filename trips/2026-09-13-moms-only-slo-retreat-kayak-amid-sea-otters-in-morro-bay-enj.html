Moms-Only SLO Retreat: Kayak Amid Sea Otters in Morro Bay; enjoy Hot Springs, Tidepooling, Coastal Hike!

CampingKayakingTidepoolingHikingMoms onlyKids & familiesMarin & Point Reyes

Imagine floating in a kelp forest as an otter drifts by with a pup on her belly. Easing into a warm mineral soak. Journaling by the water, surrounded by women who understand the beautiful weight of motherhood.

Now that school is back in session, it's time for a shift in focus — back to you. This is your invitation to reconnect with your body, your breath, and your sense of wonder on a weekend built just for moms.

October on the Central Coast has its own kind of magic — golden hills, crisp mornings, and the start of sea otter pupping season in the calm waters of the bay. It's a weekend that asks the same of you that motherhood does every day: show up, stay present,find strength you didn't know you had. This time, you get to do it for yourself.

What awaits your adventurous spirit

🦦 Kayak Among the Otters in Morro Bay

Paddle out into the calm, protected waters of iconic Morro Bay right at the start of sea otter pupping season. Guided by experienced naturalists who welcome first-timers and seasoned paddlers alike, you'll glide past otters, seals, and shorebirds beneath Morro Rock— one of the most beloved stretches of coastline in California.

♨️ Soak Beneath the Oaks

Ease into a private mineral hot spring soak — warm water, conversation, and the kind of relaxation that's hard to come by at home.

🧘 Strength &amp; Stillness

Start your mornings with gentle yoga to greet the day, then build real strength with a guided resistance training session designed for the bodies that carry us through motherhood. Resistance bands are provided to borrow — no experience or gear necessary, just show up as you are.

📓 Journaling &amp; Reflection

Slow down with guided journaling prompts designed to help you reconnect with who you are outside of "mom." Each of you will take home a handmade journal — yours to keep filling after the weekend ends.

🌲 Coastal Wonders

Between adventures, we'll take in the wild beauty of the Central Coast — the kind of scenery that resets something in you.

🏡 A Home Made for Gathering

We'll be based out of a cozy home with plenty of room to spread out, connect and unwind together (exact home confirmed once our group is locked in)

🔥 Fireside Connection

Evenings mean good food, real conversation, and probably a little too much laughter. However the group shapes up, there will be good company and stories worth telling.

🫶🏾👯🫶🏾 Your Crew Is Waiting

Come tired, come recharged, come exactly as you are. You'll be surrounded by women who understand the beautiful chaos of motherhood — solo travelers welcome, instant friendships guaranteed.
