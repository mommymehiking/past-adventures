(WaitList) Father's Day/Parent's Day Garden Fun: See Toad Hotel, Bees, Beehives + Worms! Do Animal Yoga! Plant a Seed! Eat a Flower (FREMONT)

Kids & familiesEast Bay

We are currently accepting Waitlist registrations. Please register with the applicable pricing and we will reach out to you by Thursday 11pm.

Saturday, June 14, 1015 a.m.

We will be visiting a local community garden in Fremont that grows produce using regenerative agricultural practices. They focus on building healthy soil to create a balanced ecosystem and healthier plants. Twice a week, they harvest fresh produce from their two gardens and deliver it to two local food banks. Their focus is environmental education and regenerative gardening, with the goal to inspire communities to grow their own food and get in tune with all that nature has to offer.

WHAT TO EXPECT
