Black Friday Adventure: See Monarch 🦋 Butterflies! Explore Tiny Tidepools 🦀🌟🐚 Small Cave + Beach 🏖️

TidepoolingHikingToddler friendlyKids & families

Friday, Nov. 28, 2025, 2-4:30 p.m.

You and your family are in for such a treat! We'll use this Black Friday to #OptOutside and to delight in monarch 🦋🦋🦋&nbsp;butterflies wrapping up their annual migration and hunkering down for the winter. We'll do a short hike to see the monarchs, followed by beach play and some tidepooling/crawling through a small cave!

We'll meet in the afternoon since that's when the butterflies wake up! If you have trouble making 2 p.m. and want to join later, you can! It will be easy to find us.

WHAT TO EXPECT:

🦋🦋🦋&nbsp;MONARCH BUTTERFLIES:&nbsp;We'll visit a grove where migrating monarch butterflies have nestled up for the winter! Miranda will bring her kid-friendly telescope&nbsp;🔭 so all of the littles can get a peek! We'll also scan the trees for some resident owls&nbsp;🦉🦉🦉

🦀🌟🦭🐚🏵️🦪🪸TIDEPOOLING MAGIC + BEACH PLAY:&nbsp;We'll scout for hermit crabs, sea urchins, sea anemones, blue mussels and as many seastars as we can count!! Families can also hang out on the beach&nbsp;🏖️&nbsp;while kids build sandcastles&nbsp;🏰🏰🏰&nbsp;and dams!&nbsp;

🍕🍦🍕🍦🍕&nbsp;DINNER:&nbsp;Since we're meeting in the afternoon, we'll try to organize a simple meal for the group. We’ll order cheese pizza; participants need to sign up and pay for pizza in advance. Whoever is interested can join us for ice cream after beach play!!

🖼️🌇🌊&nbsp;STUNNING VIEWS + SUNSET:&nbsp;We'll take in some stunning views and a wonderful sunset so definitely bring your nice camera&nbsp;📸📸📸

🔍 SCAVENGER HUNT:&nbsp;Embark on a fun scavenger hunt around these very special tidepools + forest!!!

DIFFICULTY:&nbsp;About 1-mile EASY hike. There are some stairs so it's best to bring a front- or backpack car-rier

WE LOOK FORWARD TO WELCOMING YOU + YOUR FAMILY ON THE TRAIL!
