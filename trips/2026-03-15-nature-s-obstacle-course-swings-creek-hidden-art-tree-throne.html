Nature's Obstacle Course! Swings! Creek! Hidden Art! Tree Thrones in East Bay Oasis! (E. Bay, EASY)

HikingKids & familiesEast Bay

Sunday, March 15, 10:30 A.M.&nbsp;&nbsp;

WHAT TO EXPECT:

A hike full of surprises in the East Bay!

*Many fun tree and rope swings for you and your kids to try
*A nature and man made obstacle course in incredible Bay and *Redwood trees (two different areas)!
*Giant Redwood stumps for the kids to climb to the top of and have all kinds of pretend play (thrones for queens &amp; kings, castles, bakeries)
*Flowing, shallow creek for kids to play in
*Hidden art for you and your family to find!
*And, a chance to meet other families and enjoy the great outdoors together!

For those who are interested, we'll go for lunch afterward in Oakland, then to a nearby chocolate shop and a bookstore.
