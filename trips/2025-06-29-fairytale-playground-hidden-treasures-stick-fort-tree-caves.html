Fairytale Playground: Hidden Treasures, Stick Fort, Tree 'Caves' + 'Thrones', Banana Slug Bonanza + Sand Pit in Shaded Redwoods (Santa Cruz)

HikingToddler friendlyKids & familiesSanta Cruz & Monterey

Sunday, June 29, 10:30 a.m.

Welcome to this Whimsical Playland in the Redwoods in the Santa Cruz Area!!

WHAT TO EXPECT:

👀🪨 MAGICAL PAINTED ROCK TREASURE HUNT! Someone has hidden dozens of painted rocks with googly eyes throughout the forest! Can you spot them all? It's like a real-life treasure hunt where every rock has personality!

🐌🌟 BANANA SLUG BONANZA! Get ready for the ultimate banana slug safari! These bright yellow forest friends are usually EVERYWHERE - hopefully, we'll see more slugs than you can count!

🐛🔍 BUG DETECTIVE MISSION! We'll become nature scientists, collecting and identifying all the cool creepy crawlies we discover!

🏰🌲 GIANT REDWOOD CASTLES! Hollow openings at the base of Redwoods create perfect fairy tale caves and hideouts. Plus, tree stump thrones fit for woodland fairies!

🏖️⛱️ SECRET SAND PIT OASIS! A hidden sandy playground tucked away in this shaded forest paradise - bring your shovels for epic digging adventures!

🏕️🪵 STICK FORT HEADQUARTERS! An amazing fort made entirely of sticks waiting for little architects to explore, rebuild, and make their own! Perfect for imaginative play and woodland adventures.

🌸🌼 WILDFLOWER TREASURE COLLECTING! Late-season blooms are still popping up everywhere. We'll enjoy nature’s bouquets!

🔍🔍🔍 SCAVENGER HUNT: Educational flash cards, checklist for families to keep! Even informative for the littlest littles

🏕️❤️🫂 COMMUNITY, FRIENDSHIP + SUPPORT: Instant 'playdate' for your kiddos! We usually have kids from toddlers up to tweens join, with all of the kids playing together. You might see old MMH/FAS friends + hopefully make some new ones, too! We also offer all kinds of support: Forgot baby wipes? Need more snacks? Solo parenting on the outing and need some extra help? We got you!

This shaded redwood oasis is the perfect natural playground where kids can dig, build, explore, hunt for treasures, and discover amazing forest creatures - all while staying cool under the giant trees!
