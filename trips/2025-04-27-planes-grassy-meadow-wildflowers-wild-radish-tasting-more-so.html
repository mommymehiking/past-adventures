Planes! Grassy Meadow! Wildflowers! Wild Radish Tasting + More (South SF)

Day tripHikingToddler friendlyKids & familiesSan Francisco

Sunday, April 27, 10:30 a.m.

Play in a grassy meadow with a view after a wander through the woods! Nestled in a community just south of San Francisco is this hidden wonderland with spectacular views of the airport. If you can tear your kiddos away from watching the planes, there are so many wildflowers (including wild radish to eat!),, a large grassy meadow with a view, and a lovely meandering trail that makes its way through the park.

WHAT TO EXPECT

✈️ 😎 Views of the airport: What kid doesn’t love watching planes taking off and landing?!

🌸🌼🪻 Wildflowers! This is my favorite time of year here with all the wildflowers starting to come out. Enjoy the flowers and the beautiful shades of green everywhere!

🪵🪨🪵 Climb! Many trees + logs to discover!

🏕️❤️🫂 Community, friendship + support: Instant 'playdate' for your kiddos! We usually have kids from toddlers up to tweens join, with all of the kids playing together. You might see old MMH friends + hopefully make some new ones, too! We also offer all kinds of support: Forgot baby wipes? Need more snacks? Solo parenting on the outing and need some extra help? We got you!

📖🔍🗺️Explore part of the Bay Area! We will include tips on what to see in this part of the Bay Area to make the outing more of a day trip adventure, if you like!
