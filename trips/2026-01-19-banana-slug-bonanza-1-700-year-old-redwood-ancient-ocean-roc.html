Banana Slug Bonanza! 1,700-Year-Old Redwood + Ancient Ocean Rocks: MLK Day Family Hike (Peninsula)

Day tripHikingToddler friendlyStroller friendlyKids & familiesPeninsulaEasy

Monday, January 19, 10:30 a.m. — 1:00 p.m.

Kids home for MLK Day? Join us for a magical winter forest adventure! We'll visit a 1,700-year-old Redwood giant where bears once scratched their backs, explore mystical fog drifting through bright green moss-covered trees, and count banana slugs by the dozens (we found 50+ last time!). After our hike, we'll refuel at the legendary Alice's Restaurant — a Bay Area icon and perfect way to complete our holiday outing.

What to expect:

🐌🍄🌿 BANANA SLUG BONANZA + WINTER WONDERS! On our last visit, we counted 50+ banana slugs! Plus, discover colorful fungi decorating the trees, vibrant green moss carpeting everything, and misty fog creating that magical winter forest vibe.

🌲🌳🪵 ANCIENT REDWOOD GIANT: We'll visit a 1,700-year-old original-growth Redwood! While most trees were logged in the late 1800s, a few incredible giants remain. Kids can marvel at these towering survivors while parents appreciate the history beneath the canopy.

🪨🌊⛰️ TAFONI TREASURES — ANCIENT OCEAN ROCKS! Explore giant sandstone boulders formed when this area was underwater! These unique Tafoni formations are a geological wonder that will spark kids' curiosity and imagination.

🎨🔍📝 Educational Fun for Families: Nature scavenger hunt with checklist to keep! Plus arts-and-craft activities for the kids with a special treat to take home. Perfect for engaging little learners!

🍔🥧🏪 ICONIC ALICE'S RESTAURANT: After our hike, we'll head to this legendary Bay Area landmark for lunch! A must-visit spot with great food and classic atmosphere.

🚼👶🛴 SUPER FAMILY-FRIENDLY: Just 2.6 miles with gradual decline, then uphill. Suitable for jogging strollers, wagons and bikes! Perfect for families with little ones.

🏕️❤️🫂 Community, Friendship + Support: Instant playdate for your kiddos! Kids from toddlers to tweens all play together. Reconnect with MMH friends and make new ones! Solo parenting? Forgot snacks? Need wipes? We've got your back!

📖🗺️🌲 Explore the Peninsula! We'll include tips on what to see in this area to make it a full day trip adventure if you'd like!
