🎉 Miranda Birthday Adventure in SF (FREE EVENT)! 🎉 Water Play, Climbing, Art, Sandcastle Building, Nature Exploring & More!

HikingToddler friendlyKids & familiesSan Francisco

📅 SUNDAY, SEPT. 7 • ⏰ 11:00 AM - 12:30 PM • 📍 San Francisco

🌊 Join us for an epic birthday celebration in San Francisco at a stunning 2-mile, 50-acre oceanfront park. I grew up in this neighborhood and am thrilled to see this incredible transformation from highway to coastal wonderland.

🎁 No presents needed - just bring yourselves!
🏖️ Join us for an epic 1.5-hour adventure! We'll explore nature, discover amazing art installations, climb giant sculptures, try out the skate track, build sandcastles, and create art from nature. The celebration wraps up with birthday cake and water play at the beach!
🌟 Open to all! Never joined one of our adventures before? This is the perfect chance to come see what the fun is all about!
📝 RSVP required: Add this outing to your cart and checkout to receive the detailed meeting point and activity guide!

ADVENTURE HIGHLIGHTS:

🌿Nature Exploration: Discover the incredible dune restoration area! We'll hunt for native plants and coastal wildlife, explore the unique ecosystem, and learn about this amazing transformation from highway to natural habitat.

🐙Giant Art Installations: Climb on Octavius the 30-foot orange octopus sculpture! Explore the climbable giraffes, discover the Instagram-worthy heart sculpture, and experience the colorful seaside art that makes this park magical.

🛹Wheels &amp; Thrills: Check out the skate track and bike obstacle course! Bring scooters, bikes, or just enjoy watching the action. There's something for every skill level and age.

🏰Beach Adventures: Build epic sandcastles, hunt for treasures in the sand, and create nature art with shells, driftwood, and beach finds. Plus splash in the waves!

🎂Birthday Celebration Finale: We'll wrap up our adventure with birthday cake and water play at the beach - the perfect way to celebrate with ocean views and new friends!
