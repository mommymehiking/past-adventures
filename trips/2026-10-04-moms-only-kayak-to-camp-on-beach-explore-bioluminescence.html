Rediscover Your Adventurous Spirit. Connect with Nature, Fellow Moms and Yourself​​​​​​​​​​​​​​

Imagine paddling on a dark, starry night, watching the water glow, sharing conversations around a campfire, journaling your thoughts, and immersing yourself in nature while being surrounded by women who understand the beauty and weight of motherhood.

Now that school has begun, it's time for a shift in focus back to you. This trip is a chance for you to discover your adventurous side and join us on a magical bioluminescent kayaking and camping adventure.

October brings a special kind of magic to the Point Reyes seashore — crisp air, golden light and the return of the bioluminescent glow to Tomales Bay. Under a canopy of stars, each stroke of a kayak paddle lights up the water with a magical glow.

Run your hands through the brilliance as you re-ignite your own sparkle and shimmer

It's an awe-inspiring journey through darkness and light — a beautiful metaphor for motherhood itself: navigating the unknown and uncovering your inner brilliance.

For this journey, all you need to do is show up and embrace the adventure, reflection and celebration in the breathtaking wilds of the Point Reyes National Seashore.

WHAT AWAITS YOUR ADVENTUROUS SPIRIT:

🛶 Bioluminescent Kayaking Adventure THIS is the season for bioluminescence and we'll be under the new moon—get ready for breathtaking magic at your fingertips! Nature's own light show is calling! Dip your paddle into Tomales Bay and watch liquid starlight dance around you. Professional guides ensure everyone—first-timer or seasoned paddler—experiences this breathtaking phenomenon safely. Prepare for pure magic.

🍕 Culinary Adventures That'll Make Your Taste Buds Sing Skip the wait, savor the moment! We've got awesome access to one of the area's most coveted bistro—famous for their crackling wood-fired pizzas, ocean-fresh oysters, and salads so incredible you'll actually want to eat every last leaf. No standing in those notorious lines; just pure culinary bliss.

But wait, there's more flavor ahead! Our digital treasure map reveals other mouth-watering spots that foodies rave about: a legendary bakery, plus artisan local cheese where each bite tells the story of this magical landscape.

🌊 Coastal Wonders That'll Take Your Breath Away

Stand where land meets endless Pacific at an iconic lighthouse that's witnessed centuries of stories.
Duck through California's rare tree tunnel—a natural cathedral that feels straight out of a fairy tale.
Lose yourself on clifftop trails where every turn reveals another jaw-dropping vista of the wild West Coast. These aren't just photo ops; they're soul-stirring moments you'll carry forever.

🏕️ Effortless Car Camping in Paradise All the joy of sleeping under stars, none of the grueling hikes. Our group-friendly site puts you steps from restrooms, hot showers, and crackling fire rings. It's camping, elevated.

🔥 Fireside Connection & Gooey S'mores As twilight paints the sky, we'll gather around dancing flames for storytelling, genuine laughter, and marshmallows toasted to golden perfection. (Weather backup: cozy gas stove gatherings that are just as magical.)

🪸 🎨 Soul-Nourishing Rituals: Yoga. Art. Journaling Greet the sunrise with gentle yoga flows. Challenge your body with heart-pumping coastal hikes. Quiet your mind with reflective journaling. Create something beautiful with your own art supplies—a tangible reminder of insights discovered and dreams rekindled.

👯 Your Tribe is Waiting Come exhausted, come energized, come exactly as you are. Whether you're craving deep restoration or can't-breathe laughter, you'll find yourself surrounded by women who understand the beautiful chaos of motherhood. Solo travelers especially welcome—instant friendships guaranteed.

Ready to trade your to-do list for kayaking amid shimmering waters, culinary delights, heart-pumping hikes and stunning coastal views and instant camaraderie and belly laughs with moms?
