Campsite Description

The campsite is on a small rolling hillside with a fire pit, plus picnic tables and food storage lockers. The ground is a mix of grass and dirt.

Modern restrooms (flush toilets, sinks with running water) are nearby.

Gear Loans

I have gear for kids and adults that you can borrow for this trip: jogging strollers, baby and toddler carriers, sleeping bags, air pads, wetsuits and more. Borrow it from Gear loans: $10 an item, for its upkeep between loans. Choose this trip when you add an item to your cart. Your guide brings it on the day; if there is no room in their car, you may need to pick it up from them before the trip.

Important Parking Note

Only 4 parking spaces are available for our campsite. You can unload your gear at the campsite first, then move your car to the lot — so you won't have to haul your stuff from a farther lot. If you're beyond the first 4 cars, you'll park in a nearby public parking area.

Important: Tent-Only Campground

No RVs, pop-ups, or camper vans

Itinerary

Friday: Check-in from 4 p.m. on. Make camp, kids enjoy arts and crafts, plus games. We'll explore the campground! Begin cooking dinner at 6:30 p.m.; later, we'll make s'mores around the campfire and possibly take in a glorious sunset!

Saturday: Breakfast at campsite then out on our day hike adventure!! We'll see awesome views and the kids will have a great time exploring old bunkers, taking awesome family photos with the Golden Gate Bridge in the background and then hanging out on the beach! In the afternoon, it's Halloween time! 🎃 Tie-dye workshop, tent decorating, painting the glow-in-the-dark tunnel, costume parade and trick-or-treating between tents. After dark: the haunted tents, the glowing tunnel and campfire ghost stories. 👻🔥

Sunday: Breakfast then break camp at 11 a.m. For those who are interested, we're going to play in the most awesome coastal playground ever, where we can get lunch from food trucks!! We say our goodbyes until the next adventure!

Weather

☀️ Mid-October is one of the best times of year in SF! It's usually sunny, with highs in the high 70s and lows in the mid 50s overnight. Please ensure that you have a season-3 sleeping bag (which is the most common one; it's great for everything except snow). I'll be keeping an eye on the weather and will advise you as we get closer to the trip.
