🌊 SPLASH ZONE ADVENTURE! 🌊

Perfect for families seeking outdoor fun, nature exploration and unforgettable bonding time on a cool beach that doesn't require driving to the coast! Toddler friendly!

Sunday, Aug. 3, 10:30 a.m.

💦 Water Fun: Crystal-clear waters where your kids can splash and make memories by San Francisco Bay!

🐚 Beach Treasure Hunt: Comb the shore for beautiful mussel shells, clam treasures, and spot playful crabs scuttling along the water's edge. Every step reveals a new discovery!

🚂 Kiddo's Choo Choo: All aboard! A trackless train offers a short 10 minute scenic ride that’s $5 per person.

🍓 Strawberries: No guarantee, but there could be thimbleberries around to snack on as we hike!

🪁 Kite Flying: Launch colorful kites into the sky when Mother Nature provides the perfect breeze and watch your family's spirits soar together!

✈️ Plane Spotting: Front-row seats to watch massive planes glide in for landing at SFO - it's like having your own personal aviation display!

🌳 Climbing Trees: Discover trees asking to be climbed (!) and natural play structures where kids can safely explore and unleash their inner adventurers!

🥾 Gentle Hike & Picnic: Take a peaceful family stroll with stunning bay views, perfect picnic spots, and refreshing ocean breezes that'll keep everyone cool and happy!

🛝 Playground: If any little ones have extra energy to expend before the drive home, there's an amazing playground with plenty of fun play structures to get those last wiggles out!

⛵ Marina Visit (Optional): Stroll through the picturesque marina and marvel at sailboats, yachts, and vessels of all shapes and sizes!

🍔🍰🏪 Local Eats Quest: We’ll offer an itinerary to sample some local treats on your way home!

🔍🔍🔍 Nature Scavenger Hunt: Educational exploration cards and checklist for families to keep! Perfect for engaging even the littlest adventurers as they discover this Half Moon Bay treasure.

🏕️❤️🫂 Community, friendship + support: Instant 'playdate' for your kiddos! We usually have kids from toddlers up to tweens join, with all of the kids playing together. You might see old MMH friends + hopefully make some new ones, too! We also offer all kinds of support: Forgot baby wipes? Need more snacks? Solo parenting on the outing and need some extra help? We got you!

📖🔍🗺️Explore part of the Bay Area! We will include tips on what to see in this part of the Bay Area to make the outing more of a day trip adventure, if you like!

NOTE: Park has a $6 entrance fee
