2/8 (SUNDAY): Waterfall Wonderland! 💦 Plus Ladybug Love 🐞, Banana Slugs 🐌 + Funghi Hunt 🍄, + Rock Climbing 🧗‍♀️

WaterfallsHikingToddler friendlyStroller friendlyKids & families

Sunday, Feb. 8, 2026, 10:30 a.m. — 12:45 p.m.

With the recent rains, our local waterfalls are FLOWING and absolutely spectacular right now! Join us for a winter wonderland adventure where we'll explore three rushing waterfalls, hunt for colorful mushrooms and leopard-spotted banana slugs, and search for clusters of ladybugs (did you know a group of ladybugs is called a "loveliness"? Perfect for Valentine's season! 💕).

WHAT TO EXPECT

Winter waterfalls in full force 💦: This is a seriously special hike since there are 3 gorgeous, rushing waterfalls - and with the recent rains, they're at their most dramatic and powerful right now! Beautiful backdrops for family photos and so fun to splash in (if you dare brave the cold!).

Funghi hunt 🍄: Try to find the turkey tail funghi growing along the logs! Winter is actually one of the best times for funghi spotting, and with all the rain we've had, they're popping up everywhere. There are quite a few other varieties as well, and we can have some fun learning the names of all of them!

Leopard spotted banana slugs 🟢: Perhaps you've seen a yellow banana slug, but have you seen a leopard-spotted GREEN banana slug?! The color of the slugs can vary depending on their diet and the amount of moisture in their environment, and the slugs in this area are rather strikingly different than their yellow cousins!

Ladybug love 🐞💕: Can you find the ladybugs? This lovely wooded area is home to ladybugs, and when they cluster on the branches of the trees and bushes, it's a sweet sight to see. Did you know that a group of ladybugs is called a "loveliness"? How perfect for this Valentine's season! We'll have a special Valentine-inspired craft for the kids to celebrate these adorable little beetles.

Rock climbing fun 🧗‍♀️: Got some adventurous climbers? There are some fun rock scrambling opportunities along the trail where the kiddos can test their climbing skills!

🔍 Scavenger Hunt: Your kids will embark on a fun scavenger hunt and get a non-edible treat!

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Meet Other Families: Connect with other adventurous families and enjoy the great outdoors together!

We look forward to welcoming you + your family on the trail!
