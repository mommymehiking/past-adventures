Summit Sisters: Power Hike & Strength Training

Seven miles and 1,500 feet of climbing, with strength training in a meadow on the way up. A training hike for moms who want to do something hard together.

SHORT. HARD. TRANSFORMATIVE.

4 Hours. 7 Miles. 1,500+ Ft elevation gain. YOU.

Sunday, Sept. 27 | 7:15 AM Sharp

Mamas — as often as we can, we lace up, show up and do something HARD together.

7 miles (not including a 1-mile walk from the parking area). 1,500+ feet of elevation gain. Resistance and strength training. And a community of women who show up for each other — every single time.

This is building real strength on a hillside, surrounded by your people and stunning nature, pushing through the voice that says stop — and choosing to keep going.

If you’re asking yourself if you can do it, I’m here to tell you that yes, you can.

TOGETHER, WE WILL DO THIS. 💪🏾🌿👊🏾

THIS IS A TRAINING PROGRAM

Designed to build something over time: hill endurance, strength, mental toughness and a community of mamas who hold each other accountable. We want the SAME group of women showing up together, time after time — because that's how real community is built. You'll know each other's names, cheer each other up the hard parts, and feel the difference that consistency makes — in your body and in your bonds.

Every time, you'll feel yourself getting stronger. The hills that took everything from you the first time? A few sessions in, you'll be having conversations on the way up.

And this program has a north star: the summit of that mountain. A 7-mile challenge we'll conquer together in late fall or early winter. Every time is a step closer. Every mile, every climb, every workout — it's all leading somewhere. And when we get there together? That's going to be something none of us will ever forget.

A note from Miranda: I am not a professional trainer or fitness instructor. What I am is a mama who loves the outdoors, knows this trail, and believes deeply in what we can accomplish together. Come ready to work hard, listen to your body, and have the time of your life.

WHAT AWAITS YOU 💥

🥾 The First Mile: THE BEAST We start with a climb. A real one. It will be hard. Your lungs will wake up fast and your legs will know they're working. But when you crest that first mile, you will feel absolutely FANTASTIC — and everything that follows will remind you why you came.

👊🏾 The Meadow: Resistance + Strength Training Right after that first mile, we arrive at a beautiful open meadow — and that's where we get to work. Resistance and strength training with your heart pumping and the open sky above you. No gym required. Just you and grit.

🌿 The Middle Miles: Your Reward After that opening climb, it’s still uphill — but a gradual one. The trail is mostly shaded and beautiful. This is where you catch your breath, find your stride, and take in views that will stop you in your tracks.

🌁 Views That Will Steal Your Breath You can see the San Francisco skyline from this trail. The Bay stretching out below you. The hills rolling in every direction. This is a reminder of how spectacular this world is — and how spectacular YOU are for being out here in it. And at our turnaround point at 3.5 miles? Incredible views that make every step worth it.

🚗 We Carpool! Heads up — the parking lot is small, so we make it work together. Carpooling is part of the experience — more time with your crew, less stress, better for everyone. We'll coordinate details when you sign up.

🍽️ Optional Post-Hike Celebratory Lunch If you've got time, we're keeping the party going! Join us for a celebratory lunch after the hike — share stories, laugh about the hard parts, and toast to what you just accomplished. You earned it, mama.

THE BIGGER PICTURE: THAT SUMMIT IS CALLING 🏔️

This program has a destination.

A mountain. 7 miles. A summit with views that go on forever. The last mile is also a beast — but by the time we get there, you will have spent months building exactly the strength and endurance you need to crush it. Every time we show up together is training for that moment.

And when we summit together? That's going to be something none of us will ever forget.
