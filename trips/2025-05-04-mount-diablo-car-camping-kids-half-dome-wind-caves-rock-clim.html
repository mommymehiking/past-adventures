CampingDay tripHikingToddler friendlyStroller friendlyKids & familiesEast BaySan Francisco

Friday, May 2 — Sunday, May 4 (DAY TRIP AVAILABLE)

Mount Diablo State Park stands as one of California's crown jewels, rising to nearly 3,900 feet in the East Bay — the second highest summit in the Bay Area!

This nature sanctuary offers visitors extraordinary experiences: spectacular wildflower displays in spring, panoramic vistas — visitors can see from the Sierra Nevada to the Golden Gate Bridge — year round, so much wildlife (!), and treasures like 50 million-year-old snail fossils and wind caves carved by powerful winds and raindrops that worked together, like nature's artists, to bore holes and tunnels through massive sandstone rocks.

As one of our young explorers put it, this place is "SO FUN!!!!" You and your family will have a terrific time exploring this corner of Mount Diablo and learning about how nature created this special place!
