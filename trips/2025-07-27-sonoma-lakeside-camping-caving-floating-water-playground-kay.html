July 25–27, 2025

SONOMA LAKESIDE CAMPING: Caving, Floating Water Playground, Kayaking, Kids' Train, Animal Barn, Fishing + More!

CampingKayakingDay tripHikingToddler friendlyKids & familiesFurther afield

Friday, July 25 — Sunday, July 27 (DAY TRIP AVAILABLE)

We’ll celebrate the end of summer at this incredible lakeside gem in Sonoma County!

WHAT WE'LL ENJOY 🥳🥳🥳

  • 🏊‍♀️💦 Splash and play on the amazing floating water playground — kids 6 years and older will have a blast bouncing and climbing on this unique aquatic adventure! 🏖️🏖️🏖️ Younger kids will get to swim in the lagoon!

  • 🛶🚣‍♀️ Paddle the crystal-clear waters with kayak, paddleboard and boat rentals available right at the lake! For kids age 7+ 🌊🌊

  • 🎣🐠 Cast a line and try your luck fishing in the well-stocked lake — who knows what you might catch! Note: A California fishing license is required for anglers 16 and older.

  • 🚂🎠 Kids paradise with a playground featuring a charming choo-choo train and classic carousel — pure childhood magic! 🚂🚂 Note: Participants will have to buy tickets for the train, carousel and barn separately

  • 🕳️⛰️ Underground adventure a half-hour’s drive away with our guided caving experience: Explore hidden wonders beneath the earth for those ready for extra excitement!

  • 🦎🐦 Discover local wildlife at the nature center and learn about the amazing ecosystem surrounding Spring Lake 🌿🌿🌿

  • 🔍🗺️Enjoy a scavenger hunt with your kids and educational activities for families

  • 🎨🖍️ Toy and arts-and-craft station for the kids when we're not out adventuring

  • 🔥🍫 Enjoy classic camping fun: Campfire, stories, s'mores, stargazing and quality time away from screens

  • 🫶🏿🫶🏾🫶🏽 Community, friendship and support: Instant 'playdate' for your kiddos! We usually have kids from toddlers up to tweens join, with all of the kids playing together! You might see old FAS friends + hopefully make some new ones, too! We offer all kinds of support: Need help setting up your tent? Solo parenting on the adventure and need some extra support? We got you!

This is our idea of SUMMER CAMP for kids, but it's for families and we get to explore different campgrounds and parks! You will make wonderful memories with your family and friends 🏕️❤️🫂🤲🏽!!
