May 17-18, 2025 | Spring Renewal: The Weekend After Mother's Day

Emerge from winter's cocoon into the vibrant renewal of spring at our exclusive moms-only retreat in the magical landscape of Big Sur. Spaces are limited to ensure an intimate, supportive experience.

A GIFT TO YOURSELF: RENEW. RESTORE. RECONNECT.

After months of tending to winter colds, managing household chaos and weathering literal storms, it's time to honor your strength and reclaim your spirit. This retreat is designed to celebrate YOU — not just as a mom, but as a person with her own dreams, desires and untapped adventurous spirit.

The timing couldn't be more perfect.:This winter's rains have transformed Big Sur into a verdant paradise, with wildflowers painting the hillsides and the Big Sur River flowing with renewed vitality. Just as nature emerges more vibrant after the rains, this retreat offers you the space to emerge more radiant after winter's challenges.

EXPERIENCE THE MAGIC

Imagine wading through the crystal waters of the Big Sur River Gorge, feeling the cool water wash away months of accumulated stress. Picture yourself breathing deeply as the sun sets over the Pacific, celebrating motherhood around a campfire with women who understand your journey.
