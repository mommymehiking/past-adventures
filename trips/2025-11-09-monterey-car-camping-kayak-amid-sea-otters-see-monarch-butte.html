November 7–9, 2025

Monterey Car Camping: Kayak🛶Amid Sea Otters 🦋 See Monarch Butterflies 🏖️ Beach, Lighthouse + Tidepools! (DAY TRIP available)

CampingKayakingTidepoolingDay tripHikingToddler friendlyKids & familiesSanta Cruz & Monterey

Friday, Nov. 7 — Sunday, Nov. 9

Experience the best of California's coast at Monterey Bay! Enjoy perfect fall weather with sunny skies while kayaking among sea otters, visiting a Monarch butterfly sanctuary, exploring tidepools and discovering historic lighthouses — all from a well-located campground with modern amenities (think flush toilets and showers!).

WHAT WE'LL ENJOY&nbsp;🥳🥳🥳

  • 🛶🦦 Kayaking in a sheltered area of Monterey Bay where families can potentially see sea otters, harbor seals, sea lions + more!!

  • 🦋🧡 Visit a Monarch Butterfly grove that is normally teeming with butterflies, but every year is different

  • 🐚⭐ Explore incredible tidepools with sea stars, crabs, mussels, sea anemones + more

  • 🗼🏠 Visit an historic lighthouse that's been restored to its time as a lighthouse that the keeper and their family lived in — one of the few open for visitors in California!

  • 🔍🗺️Enjoy a scavenger hunt with your kids and educational activities for families

  • 🎨🖍️ Toy and arts-and-craft station for the kids when we're not out adventuring

  • 🔥🍫 Enjoy classic camping fun: Campfire, stories, s'mores, stargazing and quality time away from screens

  • 🫶🏿🫶🏾🫶🏽 Community, friendship and support: Instant 'playdate' for your kiddos! We usually have kids from toddlers up to tweens join, with all of the kids playing together! You might see old MMH friends + hopefully make some new ones, too! MMH offers all kinds of support: Need help setting up your tent? Solo parenting on the adventure and need some extra support? We got you!

This is our idea of SUMMER CAMP for kids, but it's for families and we get to explore different campgrounds and parks! You will make wonderful memories with your family and friends&nbsp;🏕️❤️🫂🤲🏽!!
