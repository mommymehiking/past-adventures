Ride WILD: A Pre-Mother's Day Adventure in Golden Gate's Wilder Side 🚲🌉🌿

BikingHikingMoms onlyKids & familiesMarin & Point ReyesSan Francisco

Sunday, May 3, 2026 &middot; Sausalito &amp; Marin Headlands &middot; Half Day &middot; 9:30 AM — 1:30 PM 🗓️

When did you last feel truly WILD? 🌿

Wild like … FREE. Wild like the version of you that exists when the mom to-do list disappears and you're just a woman on a bike, breathing salt air, moving through one of the most beautiful places on Earth with nowhere else to be.

On Sunday May 3 — the weekend before Mother's Day — we're escaping into the wilder side of the Golden Gate on e-bikes. This isn't a tour. It's a reclamation. 🚲🌉

From the charming waterfront streets of Sausalito to historic bunkers with sweeping views of the San Francisco skyline, from a little-known beach bursting with spring wildflowers to a summit where the Bay, the Bridge, and the Pacific stretch out in every direction — this ride is all about perspective. Each stop a different vantage point. Each one a reminder that when you shift how you look at things — the landscape, your life — everything looks different. 🌁✨

We chose the Sunday before Mother's Day intentionally — so you can celebrate yourself before the family festivities begin. The brunches, the traditions of the actual day — that's all coming. But this? This is your WILD RIDE. 💛

Come solo and leave with a crew, or bring your person and make it a Mother's Day weekend you'll never forget. Spots are limited — Let's ride, WILD 🚲🌉🌿

WHAT MAKES THIS RIDE SO SPECIAL:

🚲 E-Bikes Open the Way — You Bring the Adventure

Good news: e-bikes are a game changer! ⚡ The electric assist handles the climbing so you can focus on the scenery, the conversation, and the pure joy of the ride. This is a paved and wide open road experience — charming streets, coastal roads, and sweeping scenic paths. Not a technical trail in sight. 🛣️

You'll get a good workout — enough to feel proud and accomplished — without it ever feeling like too much. Our expert guides will have you briefed and rolling within 20 minutes and they'll be with you the whole way. 🙌🏾

🌊 Sausalito — Where We Begin

We start along the famous Bridgeway — Sausalito's iconic waterfront road — where you'll ride past colorful houseboats bobbing in the Bay, charming cafes, and stunning views across the water to San Francisco. ⛵ It's the kind of start that makes you wonder why you don't do this every weekend.

🥪 A Charming Local Deli Stop

Our route takes us past one of Sausalito's most beloved local spots — a charming, delicious deli where you can pre-order your lunch before we head into the Headlands. 🥖☕ The perfect fuel for the adventure ahead.

🏯 Historic Bunkers — A Hidden World at the Foot of the Bridge

As we ride toward the Golden Gate Bridge, we'll pass through an area most Bay Area residents have never explored — old military bunkers tucked into the hillside, dramatic and unexpected. 🌉 From here, the views of the San Francisco skyline are absolutely breathtaking. Most people drive right past this without ever knowing it exists.

🔒 The Love Lock Viewpoint

Along the road you'll find a fence covered in locks left by visitors from around the world — heart-shaped locks, personalized locks, locks left as a symbol of love, hope, and intention. 💛 We'll stop here for photos and a moment of reflection. Each of you will receive a keepsake charm engraved with WILD — lock it to the fence as your Mother's Day intention, or take it home as a reminder of the mom who showed up for herself today. 🌿 Either way, the photos here are extraordinary. 📸

🌿 An Unexpected Beach — Wildflowers, Lunch &amp; Your Botanical Keepsake

We'll descend to a little-known beach 🏖️ — one that offers a view of the Golden Gate Bridge from an angle most people never see. Spring wildflowers will be in full bloom all around us 🌸🌼 — and this is where we'll stop for lunch, soak it all in, and create our botanical keepsake.

You'll forage native coastal plants — sagebrush, fennel, wildflowers — and press them onto a beautiful notecard printed with a special reflection prompt. 🌿🎨 Made with your own hands, in one of the most beautiful unexpected spots in the Bay Area. A tangible, meaningful memory of the day that you'll keep forever.

This time is YOURS. 💛 No agenda. No rushing. Just you, the wildflowers, the bridge in the distance, and whatever comes up when you finally give yourself permission to stop. 💭

⛰️ The Summit — Seeing the Whole Picture

We'll ride up to one of the most breathtaking viewpoints in all of California. 🌁 On one side: the Golden Gate Bridge, the Bay, and the San Francisco skyline. On the other: the Pacific Ocean stretching to the horizon. From up here, everything looks different.

That's kind of the whole point. 💛

This is where we'll pause for our final reflection prompt — about zooming out, seeing the bigger picture, and remembering that sometimes the most powerful thing you can do is step back and take in the view.

👩🏽‍🤝‍👩🏾 Your People Are Here

You'll be riding with other moms who are also saying YES to themselves this Mother's Day 💪🏾 — showing up, getting outside, and doing something that is just for them. Come solo and leave with a crew, or bring your person and make it a weekend you'll never forget. 🫶🏽🫶🏾🫶🏿

🗺️ Zero Stress — We Handle Everything

Route planning, bike fitting, gear, safety briefing — all handled. ✅ Your job is to show up in Sausalito and enjoy the ride. All updates via WhatsApp. 📱

🥂 Finish in Style in Sausalito

We'll end back in charming Sausalito — wrap up at a local spot for an optional celebratory drink on your own tab. You've earned it. 🥂

Come back to yourself. Come ride WILD. Spots are limited — Let's ride, WILD 🚲🌉🌿
