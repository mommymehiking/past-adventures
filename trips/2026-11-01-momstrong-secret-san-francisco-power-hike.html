THE CITY'S WILD SIDE 🌿

San Francisco is hiding a whole other world in plain sight. Tucked between the neighborhoods are wooded paths, canyon trails and hillsides covered in native plants that were here long before any of us. Towering trees. Wildflowers. Hawks circling overhead and hummingbirds working the blooms.

One minute you'll feel miles from anywhere — then you turn a corner and there's the skyline. That's the magic of this route.

WHAT AWAITS YOU 💥

🌲 Hidden Paths + Secret Stairways Forget the main roads. We'll climb mural-covered stairways, duck onto wooded trails and find shortcuts you'll be bragging about at school pickup.

🌼 Native Plants + City Critters Real nature, right in the city. We'll stop to meet the native plants and keep watch for the wildlife that thrives here. It's a naturalist's treasure hunt on every block.

🎨 Murals, Little Libraries + Hidden Gems Giant painted flowers, sweeping hillside murals, whimsical little free libraries — on foot, the whole city becomes an open-air gallery. Charge your phone. You'll want the photos.

📸 Sutro Tower Like You've Never Seen It Everyone knows Sutro Tower. We'll find the lesser-known spots where it shows off — towering overhead, framed by trees, peeking over the rooftops. Photo ops galore.

🏘️ Charming Neighborhoods We'll wander through some of the city's most charming corners: flower-lined front steps, colorful houses, cozy indie bookstores and the kind of character you only notice at walking speed.

🥞🍦 The TREAT A real-deal diner serving up pancakes. An awesome taqueria. An old-school ice cream shop. Bookstores worth getting lost in. After all those hills, you've earned every bite. (Bring a little spending money — and an appetite.)

🍃 Earn Your WILD Leaf Finish the challenge and take home a hand-carved wooden leaf — WILD on one side, and a nod to Sutro Tower on the other. A keepsake for the mama who climbed every hill, and a reminder of what you can do.

🚗 We Carpool! City parking is no joke, so carpooling is strongly encouraged — more time with your crew, less circling the block. We'll coordinate the meeting spot and rides when you sign up. (Planning to leave early? Let us know so we can match you with the right ride.)

A note from Miranda: I am not a professional trainer or fitness instructor. What I am is a mama who loves the outdoors, scouted every step of this route, and believes deeply in what we can accomplish together. Come ready to work hard, listen to your body, and have the time of your life.
